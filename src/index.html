Welcome to The Test Loaf

The Test Loaf is a small neighbourhood bakery that exists only on this page. It was made up to show what the AgentiCCells site factory can build from a short brief. There is no shop, no oven and no bakers, but if there were, this is what they would say:

Come in, take your time, and pick something warm. We bake in small batches through the morning, so the shelves change as the day goes on.

What we bake

Everyday breads, a few slow-proved loaves, and a small tray of pastries. See the menu for the six things we make.
